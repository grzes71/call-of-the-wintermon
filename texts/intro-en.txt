It was meant to be a routine gig.
Wommbath was unleashing his growls
on stage at Castle Wintermon.
Yet his guttural roaring echoed
deep into the ancient abyss below.
Slumbering for eons, an Elder God
heard his cry and demanded the CDs
with the greatest metal in the world.
Little did Wommbath know, however,
the albums were stolen away by
jealous gods. Now he must retrieve
and deliver them before the dawn!
